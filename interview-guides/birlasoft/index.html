Birlasoft Interview Guide (2026)

Birlasoft hires freshers through an online aptitude test (including a distinctive flowchart section), a technical interview on core languages, and an HR round. It commonly requires mathematics as an academic subject and enforces standard backlog and gap norms, with a compact three-round structure.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Aptitude Test

~90 min
Aptitude, English, and flowcharts

Around 25 questions with multiple parts including aptitude and a flowchart section, testing numerical ability, English, and logical thinking. The flowchart component is a distinctive element testing algorithmic reasoning.

Example questions
  • Numerical ability and arithmetic
  • English usage and vocabulary
  • Logical/analytical reasoning
  • Flowchart-based logic and sequencing
  • Basic algorithmic thinking
How to prepare
  • Practice flowchart-reading and logic-sequencing problems
  • Take timed aptitude mocks
  • Revise English fundamentals
  • Drill numerical reasoning
Common mistakes
  • Neglecting flowchart practice
  • Poor time management
  • Weak English basics
What they look for
  • Accurate flowchart/logic reasoning
  • Balanced numerical and verbal accuracy
  • Analytical thinking
2

Technical Interview

20-40 min
Core languages and fundamentals

Focuses on core languages such as C, C++, Java, and SQL, along with CS fundamentals and your projects. Be ready to explain code and write basic queries.

Example questions
  • C/C++/Java concepts and output prediction
  • SQL query writing
  • OOP concepts
  • DBMS and OS basics
  • Project walkthrough
How to prepare
  • Revise C, C++, Java, and SQL fundamentals
  • Practice basic query writing
  • Prepare project explanations
  • Do a mock technical interview
Common mistakes
  • Overstating language skills
  • Weak SQL
  • Poor project ownership
What they look for
  • Solid core-language knowledge
  • SQL competence
  • Genuine project depth
3

HR Interview

10-20 min
Fit and logistics

Covers motivation, communication, relocation, and joining. Usually the final, lighter step.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why Birlasoft
  • Relocation and flexibility
  • Strengths, weaknesses, goals
  • Comfort with standard terms
How to prepare
  • Prepare a concise self-introduction
  • Research Birlasoft's business
  • Have consistent flexibility answers
  • Keep documents ready
Common mistakes
  • Generic answers
  • Inconsistency across rounds
  • Appearing inflexible
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Flexibility

What to master

  • Numerical ability and reasoning
  • English usage and vocabulary
  • Flowchart and algorithmic logic
  • C and C++ fundamentals
  • Java and OOP
  • SQL and DBMS
  • Operating systems basics
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

Graduates and postgraduates who had mathematics as an academic subject; minimum 60% across academics; no backlogs; academic gap not exceeding about 1 year. Apply via the official Birlasoft careers portal. Exact criteria vary by drive.

Frequently asked questions

What is the flowchart section?

A distinctive part of Birlasoft's aptitude test that presents flowchart-based logic to assess your algorithmic and sequencing reasoning without full coding.

Is mathematics as a subject required?

Many Birlasoft drives require candidates to have had mathematics as an academic subject. Confirm the specific drive's requirement.

Which languages should I prepare?

Core languages: C, C++, Java, and SQL, along with OOP and DBMS fundamentals.

What are the gap and backlog norms?

Typically no active backlogs and an academic gap not exceeding about a year. Confirm per drive.

How many rounds are there?

Commonly three: online aptitude test, technical interview, and HR round.

Where should I apply?

Use the official Birlasoft careers portal, complete your profile, and filter for fresher/entry-level roles.
