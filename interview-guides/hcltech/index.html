HCLTech Interview Guide (2026)

HCLTech recruits engineering freshers through a straightforward three-stage process: an online aptitude test with a technical section, a technical interview, and an HR round, with reported sectional cutoffs around 70%. Its distinctive feature is the parallel TechBee early-career program that hires students right after Class 12 to work while pursuing a sponsored degree.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Aptitude Test

~60 min
Aptitude and technical MCQs

Roughly 60 questions in 60 minutes across sections of equal weight (typically English, quantitative, logical reasoning, and technical) with no negative marking. Reported sectional cutoff is around 70%, so aim for a comfortable margin per section.

Example questions
  • Quantitative aptitude: arithmetic and DI
  • Logical reasoning: series, puzzles, arrangements
  • English: grammar, vocabulary, comprehension
  • Technical MCQs: DS, OOP, OS, DBMS
  • Basic output-prediction questions
How to prepare
  • Take timed 60-in-60 mocks
  • Revise technical MCQ topics (DS, OOP, OS, DBMS)
  • Drill quant and reasoning daily
  • Strengthen English fundamentals
Common mistakes
  • Missing the ~70% sectional cutoff in one weak area
  • Ignoring the technical section
  • Poor pacing at one minute per question
What they look for
  • Clearing every sectional cutoff
  • Balanced accuracy
  • Strong technical MCQ performance
2

Technical Interview

20-40 min
CS fundamentals and projects

Draws from the same DS, OOP, OS, and DBMS syllabus as the test's technical section, plus resume projects and one programming language. Straightforward but you must defend what you claim.

Example questions
  • Explain a data structure and where you used it
  • OOP concepts with examples
  • SQL query and DBMS normalization
  • OS basics (process/thread, scheduling at a basic level)
  • Project walkthrough
How to prepare
  • Revise DS, OOP, OS, and DBMS thoroughly
  • Prepare crisp project explanations
  • Practice basic SQL and dry-running code
  • Do a mock technical interview
Common mistakes
  • Overstating resume skills
  • Weak project ownership
  • Being unable to write basic SQL
What they look for
  • Solid fundamentals
  • Genuine project depth
  • Clear explanations
3

HR Interview

10-20 min
Fit, motivation, and logistics

Covers communication, motivation, relocation, shift flexibility, and joining logistics. Generally not heavily eliminatory unless communication is weak or answers are inconsistent.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why HCLTech and this role
  • Relocation and shift readiness
  • Strengths, weaknesses, and goals
  • Comfort with standard terms
How to prepare
  • Prepare a concise self-introduction
  • Research HCLTech's business
  • Have consistent flexibility answers
  • Keep documents ready
Common mistakes
  • Generic, unresearched answers
  • Inconsistency across rounds
  • Appearing inflexible
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Flexibility and professionalism

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude
  • Logical reasoning
  • English/verbal ability
  • Data structures
  • OOP concepts
  • Operating systems
  • DBMS and SQL
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

B.E/B.Tech with at least 60% aggregate across Class 10, Class 12, and the degree, and no active backlogs. Eligible branches typically include CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, and often Mechanical. Apply only through official channels (hcltech.com/careers or AMCAT). Exact criteria vary by drive.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sectional cutoff?

Reported sectional cutoffs are around 70%, roughly 11 correct out of 15 per section. Aim for 12-13 to be safe, since you must clear every section.

Is there negative marking?

No, so attempt all questions within the tight one-minute-per-question pace.

What is TechBee?

TechBee is HCLTech's early-career program that hires students after Class 12 to work in IT while pursuing a sponsored or subsidized degree through partner universities.

Which subjects appear in the technical interview?

The same DS, OOP, OS, and DBMS syllabus as the test's technical section, plus your resume projects and one language.

Do non-CS branches qualify?

Often yes: ECE, EEE, and sometimes Mechanical are eligible alongside CSE/IT, though preparation on CS fundamentals is still expected.

How do I avoid application scams?

Apply only through hcltech.com/careers or the official AMCAT platform. Any site charging a registration fee is not an official channel.
