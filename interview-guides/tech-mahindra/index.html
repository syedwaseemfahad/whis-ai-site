Tech Mahindra Interview Guide (2026)

Tech Mahindra's fresher process is multi-stage: an online aptitude test, a technical-plus-psychometric test, an AI-based communication assessment, and technical and HR interviews, with an optional SuperCoder challenge for top performers. Each online section is an independent elimination gate, and the AI-scored spoken-English module makes communication a formal, non-negotiable step.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Aptitude Test

~140 min
Aptitude screening

A large aptitude block (around 75 questions) covering quantitative, logical reasoning, and verbal ability, with no negative marking. Sectional cutoffs apply, so weak areas cannot be masked by strong ones.

Example questions
  • Quantitative: arithmetic, DI, number systems
  • Logical reasoning: series, puzzles, arrangements
  • Verbal ability and comprehension
  • Data sufficiency style items
  • Grammar and vocabulary
How to prepare
  • Take timed sectional mocks
  • Build quant speed with mental math
  • Practice reasoning puzzles daily
  • Revise grammar and RC
Common mistakes
  • Missing a sectional cutoff
  • Poor pacing over the long test
  • Neglecting verbal
What they look for
  • Balanced sectional accuracy
  • Endurance over a long test
  • Reasoning strength
2

Technical + Psychometric Test

~65 min
CS fundamentals and personality

Technical MCQs (around 26 questions) covering CS fundamentals, sometimes with a coding question that awards partial credit per passing test case, plus a psychometric/personality component. Cutoffs are independent.

Example questions
  • OOP and language fundamentals MCQs
  • OS/DBMS basics
  • A coding question with partial-credit scoring
  • Data structures MCQs
  • Psychometric situational/personality items
How to prepare
  • Revise CS fundamentals MCQs
  • Practice a few easy coding problems for partial credit
  • Answer psychometric items honestly and consistently
  • Do technical mocks
Common mistakes
  • Skipping the coding question entirely (partial credit is available)
  • Inconsistent psychometric answers
  • Weak fundamentals
What they look for
  • Solid CS fundamentals
  • Some working coding logic
  • Consistent personality profile
3

AI-Based Communication Assessment

~20 min
Spoken English fluency

An automated, AI-scored module (around 18 questions) evaluating pronunciation, fluency, listening, and grammar. It is a real elimination gate; a strong aptitude score does not offset weak communication.

Example questions
  • Read-aloud passages
  • Listen-and-respond tasks
  • Sentence construction and grammar
  • Describe a situation aloud
  • Fluency and pronunciation items
How to prepare
  • Practice reading English aloud clearly and steadily
  • Record and review your pace and clarity
  • Revise grammar
  • Do daily spoken-English drills
Common mistakes
  • Speaking too fast or unclearly
  • Long pauses hurting fluency scores
  • Grammar slips
What they look for
  • Clear pronunciation
  • Good fluency
  • Accurate grammar
4

Technical Interview

15-45 min
Fundamentals, projects, and problem-solving

A one-on-one or panel round covering OOP, OS, and topics from your resume (sometimes ML if listed), plus one to two easy DSA questions to gauge problem-solving.

Example questions
  • Resume project deep-dive
  • OOP and OS concepts
  • One to two easy DSA problems
  • Language you claim on your resume
  • A domain topic you listed (e.g., ML)
How to prepare
  • Revise OOP, OS, and one language
  • Solve easy DSA problems
  • Prepare project explanations
  • Only list skills you can defend
Common mistakes
  • Listing skills you cannot back up
  • Freezing on an easy DSA question
  • Weak project ownership
What they look for
  • Solid fundamentals
  • Working problem-solving
  • Genuine project depth
5

HR Interview

10-20 min
Fit and logistics

Covers motivation, relocation, shift flexibility, and joining logistics. Usually the final, lighter step unless communication or fit concerns arise.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why Tech Mahindra
  • Relocation and shift readiness
  • Strengths, weaknesses, goals
  • Comfort with standard terms
How to prepare
  • Prepare a concise self-introduction
  • Research Tech Mahindra's business
  • Have consistent flexibility answers
  • Keep documents ready
Common mistakes
  • Generic answers
  • Inconsistency across rounds
  • Appearing inflexible
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Flexibility

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude and DI
  • Logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability and grammar
  • Spoken English fluency
  • CS fundamentals MCQs (OOP, OS, DBMS)
  • Easy DSA and one language
  • Psychometric consistency
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

B.E/B.Tech in any branch (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil commonly eligible); typically the most recent pass-out batch, with some listings accepting graduates within 12-18 months. Standard 60% and no-active-backlog norms usually apply. Exact criteria vary by drive.

Frequently asked questions

Is the communication assessment really eliminatory?

Yes. The AI-scored communication module has its own cutoff, so weak spoken English can end your candidacy regardless of aptitude or coding scores.

Should I attempt the coding question if I am unsure?

Yes. The technical section's coding question awards partial credit per passing test case, so even a partial solution helps.

What is the SuperCoder challenge?

It is an optional challenge for top performers that can route strong coders toward a premium fresher track with higher pay.

How should I handle the psychometric test?

Answer honestly and consistently. It profiles your work style, and contradictory answers are flagged.

Which batches are eligible?

Primarily the most recent pass-out batch, though some drives accept graduates within roughly 12-18 months. Confirm per listing.

How long does the process take?

Typically about 2-4 weeks from the test to the offer, depending on the drive.
