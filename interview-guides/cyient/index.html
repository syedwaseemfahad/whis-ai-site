Cyient Interview Guide (2026)

Cyient hires freshers across IT/software and engineering/embedded roles through a written aptitude-plus-technical test, one or more technical interviews, and an HR round. For embedded roles the technical bar centers on C, C++ and Embedded C; for IT roles it centers on standard CS fundamentals and one language.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Written / Online Assessment

~60-90 min
Aptitude and role-specific technical fundamentals

A test combining aptitude/logical reasoning with a technical section matched to the role: C/C++/Embedded C and engineering fundamentals for embedded, or standard CS and light coding for IT. Some roles add a coding module.

Example questions
  • Quant and logical reasoning MCQs
  • C/C++ output and Embedded C concepts (embedded roles)
  • OOP/DBMS/DSA MCQs (IT roles)
  • Basic engineering fundamentals for the domain
  • A short coding task where applicable
How to prepare
  • Practice aptitude and logical reasoning sets
  • Revise C/C++ and Embedded C (embedded) or CS fundamentals (IT)
  • Solve a few easy coding problems if coding is included
  • Take a timed mock
Common mistakes
  • Not matching prep to your role (embedded vs IT)
  • Weak C/pointer fundamentals
  • Poor time management
What they look for
  • Balanced aptitude accuracy
  • Correct role-relevant fundamentals
  • Clean C/coding reasoning
2

Technical Interview

~30-45 min
Programming, engineering concepts and projects

Interviewers assess programming knowledge, core engineering/CS concepts, problem-solving and your resume projects. Embedded roles focus on C, microcontrollers and hardware-software basics; IT roles on DSA, DBMS and OOP.

Example questions
  • C concepts, pointers and memory (embedded)
  • Microcontroller/peripheral basics (embedded)
  • DSA, DBMS and OOP (IT)
  • Project walkthrough and your contribution
  • A problem-solving question in your domain
How to prepare
  • Revise C/Embedded C or CS fundamentals to spoken depth
  • Prepare project explanations with your exact role
  • For embedded, revise microcontrollers and protocols
  • For IT, practice DSA and SQL
Common mistakes
  • Bluffing on domain fundamentals
  • Weak project ownership
  • Definitions without examples
What they look for
  • Solid domain fundamentals
  • Clear problem-solving
  • Project ownership
3

HR Interview

~20-30 min
Communication, fit and logistics

A conversational round on communication, teamwork, adaptability, career goals and organizational fit, plus logistics like location. Genuine interest in Cyient's engineering-services domain helps.

Example questions
  • Why Cyient and its engineering/IT domain
  • A teamwork or adaptability story
  • Career goals
  • Relocation and joining readiness
  • Strengths and a growth area
How to prepare
  • Research Cyient's engineering-services and geospatial/aerospace domains
  • Prepare STAR behavioral stories
  • Rehearse an authentic 'Why Cyient'
  • Prepare honest logistics answers
Common mistakes
  • No domain awareness
  • Generic motivation
  • No behavioral examples
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Domain interest
  • Adaptability

What to master

  • Aptitude and logical reasoning
  • C, C++ and Embedded C (embedded roles)
  • Microcontrollers and protocols (embedded)
  • DSA, DBMS and OOP (IT roles)
  • One programming language depth
  • SQL basics
  • Resume project depth
  • STAR behavioral storytelling

Eligibility

Typically 60%+ across academics with no active backlogs; branch depends on role (ECE/EEE for embedded, CSE/IT for software). Confirm exact cutoffs and role fit per drive.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cyient hire for embedded and IT both?

Yes. Embedded/engineering roles focus on C, C++ and Embedded C plus hardware basics; IT/software roles focus on DSA, DBMS and OOP.

How many rounds are there?

Typically three to four: a written/online assessment, one or more technical interviews, and an HR round.

Is the technical round competitive DSA?

No. It is fundamentals and role-specific problem-solving, plus your projects.

What branches qualify?

ECE/EEE commonly for embedded and CSE/IT for software, though it varies by role and drive.

Is coding always in the test?

It depends on the role; some drives include a coding module and others are aptitude-plus-technical MCQ only.

What does Cyient do?

It is an engineering and technology services company spanning aerospace, geospatial, communications and digital, useful context for the HR round.
