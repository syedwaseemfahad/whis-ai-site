DXC Technology Interview Guide (2026)

DXC Technology hires freshers for IT-services roles through a three-round process: a proctored AMCAT-style online test (aptitude, verbal and core-CS technical), one or more technical interviews, and an HR/behavioral round. The bar is fundamentals-solid across C/C++, DBMS, OS and computer organization, not competitive DSA.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Test (AMCAT)

~100 min
Aptitude, verbal reasoning and core-CS technical, webcam-proctored

A webcam-proctored AMCAT test with an aptitude-and-verbal section (conditions/assumptions, blood relations, time-and-distance, synonyms/antonyms, comprehension) and a technical section on C, C++, DBMS, computer organization and OS. Some drives add a coding module.

Example questions
  • Logical conditions/assumptions and blood-relation puzzles
  • Time-and-distance and arithmetic word problems
  • Synonyms, antonyms and comprehension
  • C/C++ output and OS/CO concept MCQs
  • DBMS query and concept questions
How to prepare
  • Practice AMCAT-pattern aptitude on PrepInsta free sets
  • Revise C/C++ output, OS and computer organization
  • Drill verbal reasoning and vocabulary
  • Take one timed proctored-style mock
Common mistakes
  • Underestimating the computer-organization and OS MCQs
  • Weak vocabulary hurting the verbal section
  • Poor time management across sections
What they look for
  • Balanced aptitude and technical accuracy
  • Correct C/C++ output reasoning
  • Good verbal performance
2

Technical Interview

~30-45 min
CS fundamentals, one language and projects

A fundamentals round on C/C++ or your chosen language, OOP, DBMS, OS and DSA basics, plus your resume projects. Depth is explanation-level; clear correct basics beat obscure trivia.

Example questions
  • Explain OOP concepts with examples
  • A basic DSA operation and complexity
  • DBMS keys, normalization and a simple query
  • An OS concept (process vs thread, scheduling)
  • Project walkthrough and contribution
How to prepare
  • Revise OOP, DBMS, OS and DSA to spoken depth
  • Practice a few simple coding problems
  • Rehearse every resume project
  • Prepare simple SQL queries
Common mistakes
  • Bluffing on OS/DBMS gaps
  • Definitions without examples
  • Weak project ownership
What they look for
  • Clear, correct fundamentals
  • Good examples
  • Honest scope of knowledge
3

HR / Behavioral Interview

~20-30 min
Communication, fit and logistics

A conversational round on motivation, teamwork, adaptability, career goals and logistics like relocation, service agreement and shifts. Communication and stability of intent matter.

Example questions
  • Why DXC and IT services
  • A teamwork or adaptability story
  • Career goals
  • Relocation, shifts and service-agreement readiness
  • Strengths and a growth area
How to prepare
  • Research DXC's IT-services business
  • Prepare STAR behavioral stories
  • Rehearse an authentic 'Why DXC'
  • Prepare honest logistics answers
Common mistakes
  • Generic motivation
  • No behavioral examples
  • Hesitation on service agreement/relocation
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Adaptability
  • Genuine, stable intent

What to master

  • AMCAT aptitude and logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability and vocabulary
  • C/C++ and output prediction
  • OS and computer organization
  • DBMS and SQL basics
  • OOP and DSA fundamentals
  • Resume project depth
  • STAR behavioral storytelling

Eligibility

Typically BE/B.Tech with 60% and no active backlogs; patterns vary by region and year. Confirm the current process on DXC's careers portal or with your recruiter, as fresher formats change.

Frequently asked questions

What platform is the online test on?

Commonly AMCAT (Aspiring Minds), webcam-proctored, running around 100 minutes across aptitude/verbal and core-CS technical sections.

How technical is the interview?

Fundamentals-focused: C/C++ or your language, OOP, DBMS, OS and DSA basics, plus projects. It is not competitive DSA.

Is there a coding round?

Some drives add a coding module to the online test; interviews may include small coding or dry-runs. Confirm per drive.

Does DXC have a service agreement?

Some fresher offers include a bond or service agreement. Ask your recruiter and be ready to discuss it in HR.

Do only CS branches qualify?

Typically engineering branches with 60%+, but patterns vary by region and drive.

How many rounds?

Usually three: online test, technical interview and HR/behavioral.
