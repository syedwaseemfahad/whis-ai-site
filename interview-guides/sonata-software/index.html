Sonata Software Interview Guide (2026)

Sonata Software (IT services and its Platformation focus) hires freshers through an online assessment (aptitude/verbal-heavy with technical and sometimes coding), followed by up to three technical interviews and an HR/managerial round. The technical bar emphasizes core CS subjects and practical application over competitive DSA.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Assessment

~60-90 min
Aptitude, verbal, technical and sometimes coding

A multi-section test often weighted toward verbal (synonyms/antonyms, sentence correction, paragraph questions) plus logical reasoning, quantitative, and a technical/coding block. Sections evaluate both fundamentals and communication readiness.

Example questions
  • Synonyms, antonyms and sentence correction
  • Paragraph-based reading questions
  • Logical reasoning and quantitative MCQs
  • CS-fundamentals MCQs (OOP/DBMS)
  • A short coding task where included
How to prepare
  • Drill verbal ability, especially grammar and comprehension
  • Practice logical and quantitative sets
  • Revise OOP/DBMS MCQs
  • Solve a few easy coding problems
Common mistakes
  • Underestimating the heavy verbal weighting
  • Weak grammar/vocabulary
  • Poor time management
What they look for
  • Strong verbal performance
  • Balanced technical accuracy
  • Clean coding where included
2

Technical Interviews (up to 3)

~30-45 min each
Core CS, one language and projects

One to three technical rounds covering core CS subjects, a programming language, DBMS/SQL, and your resume projects, with emphasis on practical application. Later rounds may add scenario or design-thinking questions.

Example questions
  • OOP concepts and one language depth
  • DSA approach and complexity
  • SQL query and DBMS concepts
  • Project walkthrough and your contribution
  • A practical problem-solving scenario
How to prepare
  • Revise core CS to spoken-explanation depth
  • Practice DSA problems and SQL queries
  • Rehearse resume projects deeply
  • Prepare to reason about practical trade-offs
Common mistakes
  • Bluffing on fundamentals
  • Weak project ownership
  • No practical application in answers
What they look for
  • Solid core CS
  • Practical problem-solving
  • Project ownership
3

HR / Managerial Interview

~20-30 min
Communication, fit and career goals

A final round (managerial and/or HR) assessing motivation, cultural fit, communication and logistics. Difficulty is generally medium and composure carries it.

Example questions
  • Why Sonata and IT services
  • Career goals
  • A teamwork or challenge story
  • Relocation and joining readiness
  • Strengths and a growth area
How to prepare
  • Research Sonata Software and its Platformation approach
  • Prepare STAR behavioral stories
  • Rehearse an authentic 'Why Sonata'
  • Prepare honest logistics answers
Common mistakes
  • Generic motivation
  • No behavioral examples
  • Weak communication
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine fit
  • Logistical readiness

What to master

  • Verbal ability and grammar
  • Logical and quantitative reasoning
  • OOP and one language depth
  • DSA fundamentals
  • DBMS and SQL
  • CS-fundamentals MCQs
  • Resume project depth
  • STAR behavioral storytelling

Eligibility

Typically 60%+ across academics with no active backlogs; CSE/IT and allied branches for software roles. Timelines vary from a few weeks to over a month. Confirm cutoffs per drive.

Frequently asked questions

Why is verbal ability so important at Sonata?

Its online assessment is often verbal-heavy (synonyms, sentence correction, paragraph questions), so strong grammar and comprehension are a real differentiator.

How many technical rounds are there?

Up to three technical rounds plus an HR/managerial round, though many drives run fewer.

Is competitive DSA required?

No. The focus is core CS subjects and practical application, not hard competitive problems.

Is there always a coding round?

It varies by drive; some online assessments include a coding block and others are MCQ-only.

How long is the process?

Timelines vary significantly, from a few weeks to over a month.

What is Platformation?

Sonata's platform-centric modernization approach; showing awareness of it helps in the HR/managerial round.
