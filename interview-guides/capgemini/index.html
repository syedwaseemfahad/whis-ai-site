Capgemini Interview Guide (2026)

Capgemini's fresher screen is unusually format-heavy: a pseudocode MCQ section, an English communication test, a game-based cognitive aptitude section, and a behavioral (PowerSkills) test, all as independent elimination gates, followed by technical and HR interviews. The distinctive part is the game-based aptitude, where randomly selected cognitive games replace traditional reasoning MCQs.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Pseudocode MCQ

~25 min
Algorithmic thinking without syntax

Around 25 questions testing your ability to trace pseudocode logic, predict output, and understand loops, conditionals, and basic data structures without any specific language syntax.

Example questions
  • Trace the output of a loop-based pseudocode block
  • Predict the result of nested conditionals
  • Identify what a small algorithm computes
  • Complexity or step-count reasoning
  • Basic array/string operations in pseudocode
How to prepare
  • Practice pseudocode tracing on GeeksforGeeks and free banks
  • Strengthen core programming logic (loops, conditions, arrays)
  • Do timed pseudocode sets
  • Review basic data structures conceptually
Common mistakes
  • Confusing language-specific habits with pseudocode conventions
  • Rushing and misreading loop bounds
  • Weak grasp of nested logic
What they look for
  • Accurate output tracing
  • Strong logical reasoning about code
  • Speed on standard patterns
2

English Communication Test

~30 min
Grammar, vocabulary, and comprehension

About 30 questions on vocabulary, grammar, and reasoning: synonyms/antonyms, subject-verb agreement, modifiers, voice conversion, and critical reasoning. It is a real gate.

Example questions
  • Synonym/antonym and vocabulary items
  • Subject-verb agreement and tense correction
  • Active-passive voice conversion
  • Sentence improvement and modifiers
  • Reading comprehension and critical reasoning
How to prepare
  • Revise core grammar rules systematically
  • Build vocabulary with daily word lists
  • Practice error-spotting and RC
  • Do timed English mocks
Common mistakes
  • Underestimating grammar depth
  • Weak vocabulary
  • Rushing comprehension passages
What they look for
  • Strong grammar accuracy
  • Good vocabulary
  • Careful reading
3

Game-Based Aptitude

~27 min
Cognitive ability via games

Four cognitive games randomly selected from a pool, testing pattern recognition, cognitive processing speed, and deductive logic. There is no fixed question bank to memorize; you must adapt on the spot.

Example questions
  • Pattern-recognition sequencing tasks
  • Speed-and-accuracy reaction games
  • Deductive logic and rule-inference puzzles
  • Memory or spatial reasoning games
  • Adaptive difficulty tasks
How to prepare
  • Play sample cognitive/aptitude games to get comfortable with the format
  • Practice quick pattern recognition and mental agility
  • Do reaction-speed and logic puzzles
  • Stay calm and read each game's rules quickly
Common mistakes
  • Panicking because there is no memorizable question bank
  • Sacrificing accuracy for speed or vice versa
  • Not reading each game's instructions carefully
What they look for
  • Fast, accurate pattern recognition
  • Adaptability to unfamiliar tasks
  • Balanced speed and precision
4

Behavioral / PowerSkills Test

~20-25 min
Personality and workplace fit

An untimed situational/personality assessment measuring workplace behavior and traits. Answer consistently and honestly; there are no trick right answers, but contradictory responses are flagged.

Example questions
  • How you respond to workplace scenarios
  • Teamwork and collaboration preferences
  • Handling deadlines and pressure
  • Learning orientation and adaptability
  • Integrity and reliability items
How to prepare
  • Answer honestly and consistently
  • Reflect on your genuine work style beforehand
  • Avoid giving contradictory answers to similar items
  • Lean toward collaborative, reliable, growth-oriented responses
Common mistakes
  • Trying to game the test with contradictory 'ideal' answers
  • Overthinking simple preference questions
  • Inconsistency across similar items
What they look for
  • Consistent, honest responses
  • Collaborative and reliable orientation
  • Adaptability
5

Technical + HR Interview

20-45 min
Fundamentals, projects, and fit

After clearing all gates, candidates face technical and HR interviews covering resume projects, one language, CS basics, motivation, and joining logistics. Higher cutoffs can route you to the Senior Analyst track.

Example questions
  • Project walkthrough and your role
  • OOP and one language in depth
  • DBMS/SQL basics
  • Why Capgemini and career goals
  • Relocation and flexibility
How to prepare
  • Revise one language, OOP, and DBMS
  • Prepare project explanations
  • Research Capgemini's business
  • Rehearse HR answers
Common mistakes
  • Overstating resume skills
  • Generic HR answers
  • Weak project ownership
What they look for
  • Solid fundamentals
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation

What to master

  • Pseudocode and programming logic
  • English grammar, vocabulary, and comprehension
  • Cognitive aptitude (pattern recognition, logic, speed)
  • Behavioral consistency
  • One programming language and OOP
  • DBMS and SQL
  • Core CS fundamentals
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

Minimum 60% aggregate in your qualifying degree (BE/B.Tech/MCA/M.Sc or equivalent); full-time programs only (correspondence/part-time not eligible); no active backlogs at assessment and joining. Exact criteria vary by drive.

Frequently asked questions

What is the game-based aptitude section?

It replaces traditional reasoning MCQs with four cognitive games drawn randomly from a pool, testing pattern recognition, processing speed, and deductive logic. You cannot memorize a question bank; you adapt during play.

Are all sections elimination gates?

Yes. Failing any one section (pseudocode, English, game-based aptitude) exits you from the process regardless of your other scores.

How should I approach the PowerSkills/behavioral test?

Answer honestly and consistently. It is untimed and looks for reliability and collaboration; contradictory answers to similar questions are flagged.

Do I need coding beyond pseudocode?

The screen focuses on pseudocode MCQs rather than a full coding round, but the technical interview may still probe your language and simple logic.

What are the salary tracks?

Capgemini runs an Analyst track for standard intake and a higher Senior Analyst track for candidates clearing higher cutoffs across sections.

Do backlogs matter?

You typically need no active backlogs at assessment and joining, and only full-time programs are eligible. Confirm per drive.
