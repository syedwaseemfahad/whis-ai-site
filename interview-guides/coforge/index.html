Coforge Interview Guide (2026)

Coforge hires freshers as Graduate Engineer Trainees through an online assessment (aptitude plus core technical MCQs), a technical interview focused on projects and your preferred language, and an HR round, typically followed by a multi-month training program. It filters across more stages than some peers, with genuine attention to project and coding depth.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Assessment

~60-90 min
Aptitude and technical MCQs

Sections on quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, and core technical MCQs. Acts as the main screening filter for GET roles.

Example questions
  • Quantitative aptitude and DI
  • Logical reasoning: series and puzzles
  • Verbal ability and comprehension
  • Core technical MCQs (DS, OOP, DBMS, OS)
  • Basic output-prediction questions
How to prepare
  • Take timed aptitude mocks
  • Revise technical MCQ topics
  • Practice reasoning and quant daily
  • Strengthen verbal fundamentals
Common mistakes
  • Neglecting the technical MCQ section
  • Poor time management
  • Weak verbal
What they look for
  • Balanced accuracy
  • Strong technical MCQ performance
  • Speed
2

Technical Interview

20-45 min
Projects, coding, and fundamentals

A deep dive into your projects, internships, and your preferred language (Java, .NET, etc.), plus CS fundamentals and some coding. Coforge expects you to genuinely know what you built.

Example questions
  • Detailed project and internship walkthrough
  • Your preferred language in depth (Java/.NET)
  • OOP, DBMS, and OS concepts
  • A coding or logic problem
  • Technology-choice justification
How to prepare
  • Prepare deep, honest project explanations
  • Revise your primary language thoroughly
  • Practice coding and CS fundamentals
  • Do a mock technical interview
Common mistakes
  • Shallow project knowledge
  • Overstating your preferred language skills
  • Weak coding under questioning
What they look for
  • Genuine project and internship depth
  • Solid language and fundamentals
  • Clear technical reasoning
3

HR Interview

10-20 min
Goals, fit, and logistics

Discusses career goals, relocation willingness, salary expectations, and joining, plus acceptance of the training program terms.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why Coforge and career goals
  • Relocation and flexibility
  • Salary expectations
  • Comfort with the training/bond terms
How to prepare
  • Prepare a concise self-introduction
  • Research Coforge's business
  • Have consistent flexibility answers
  • Keep documents ready
Common mistakes
  • Generic answers
  • Unrealistic salary expectations for a fresher
  • Inconsistency across rounds
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Flexibility

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude and DI
  • Logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability
  • Your preferred language (Java/.NET)
  • Core DSA and coding
  • OOP and DBMS
  • Operating systems basics
  • Project and internship depth

Eligibility

B.E/B.Tech and postgraduates (MCA/M.Tech), commonly from recent batches (e.g., 2024-2026), including freshers with 0-1 year experience for GET roles. Standard academic and backlog norms usually apply. Apply via careers.coforge.com. Exact criteria vary by drive.

Your prep plan

  • Days 1-3: Aptitude diagnostic and weak-topic rebuild
  • Days 4-5: Verbal and reasoning practice
  • Days 6-8: Technical MCQs and your preferred-language revision
  • Days 9-10: Full aptitude+technical mock
  • Days 11-12: Deep project prep and coding practice
  • Days 13-14: Mock technical and HR interviews
  • Day 1: Aptitude mock plus technical MCQ and language revision
  • Day 2 morning: Coding practice and deep project prep
  • Day 2 afternoon: HR answer rehearsal and document check

Frequently asked questions

What role do freshers get at Coforge?

Most freshers are hired as Graduate Engineer Trainees (GET), usually followed by a multi-month training program before deployment.

How deep is the technical interview?

Fairly deep on your actual projects, internships, and preferred language (Java, .NET, etc.), so be ready to defend everything on your resume.

Which batches are eligible?

Commonly recent batches such as 2024-2026, including freshers with up to about a year of experience. Confirm per drive.

Where should I apply?

Use the official portal at careers.coforge.com, build a complete profile, and read each job description carefully.

How coding-heavy is it?

Expect core technical MCQs in the online round and coding/logic plus project depth in the interview. Solid fundamentals and one strong language are key.

Is there a training program?

Yes, selected candidates typically go through a training program (reported around four months) before full deployment.
