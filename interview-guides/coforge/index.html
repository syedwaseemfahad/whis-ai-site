Coforge Interview Guide (2026)

Hires Graduate Engineer Trainees via a Mettl-based assessment (aptitude, coding, verbal) followed by technical and HR interviews.

ModerateAssessment → interviews (2–4 weeks)Updated Sep 2026

The hiring process

Applications are screened, then a Mettl online assessment covering aptitude, coding, and verbal shortlists candidates for one or more technical interviews and a final HR round.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online assessment (Mettl)~60–90 minAptitude, logical reasoning, quant, coding, verbal
Technical interview~30–45 minCore CS, coding/scenario problems, project depth
HR interview~15–30 minCommunication, motivation, cultural fit

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • Logical reasoning and quant
  • Easy–medium coding
  • OOP and DBMS
  • One language in depth
  • Scenario-based problem solving
  • Project explanation

Eligibility

Open to B.E./B.Tech, MCA, and M.Tech; Coforge does not always publish a fixed CGPA/percentage cutoff, so a strong academic record and projects help. Varies by drive.

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–3: Aptitude and logical reasoning practice
  • Days 4–6: Easy–medium coding in your main language
  • Days 7–9: Revise OOP, DBMS, and CS fundamentals
  • Days 10–12: Practice scenario/problem-solving questions and polish resume
  • Days 13–14: Full mock assessment plus mock technical and HR

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: Timed aptitude plus 8–10 coding problems and verbal practice
  • Day 2: Revise OOP/DBMS, rehearse project story and one mock interview

FAQ

Which platform is the test on?

Reports say the online assessment is typically conducted on Mettl and includes aptitude, coding, and verbal sections.

Is there a fixed CGPA cutoff?

Not always published. Coforge often weighs overall profile, projects, and problem solving rather than a single strict number, but always check the drive notification.

How selective is the shortlist?

In some campus drives only a couple hundred of roughly a thousand applicants reach interviews, so the assessment is the key filter.
