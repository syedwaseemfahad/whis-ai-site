Deloitte (India) Interview Guide (2026)

Deloitte India (USI/consulting-tech) hires freshers mainly for analyst and associate-engineer tracks through a short, fast-moving funnel: an online assessment followed by a single combined technical-plus-HR interview. As of 2026 the older Group Discussion and JAM elimination rounds have largely been dropped, so the online test and one interview carry almost all the weight.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Assessment

~90-95 min
Aptitude, English, CS fundamentals and light coding under a timer

A proctored MCQ-heavy test split across English/verbal, quantitative and logical reasoning, and computer fundamentals, often with 2 short coding problems for tech roles. There is typically no negative marking, so attempt everything. Sections are usually individually timed, so pace-per-section matters more than any single hard question.

Example questions
  • Data interpretation from a small table or chart
  • Time-speed-distance or work-rate word problems
  • Sentence correction and reading-comprehension inference
  • Output-prediction on a short C/Java/SQL snippet
  • An easy string or array manipulation coding task
How to prepare
  • Drill quant and logical reasoning free sets on IndiaBix and GeeksforGeeks aptitude pages
  • Solve 30-40 easy array/string problems on LeetCode or HackerRank
  • Revise C/Java output questions and basic SQL SELECT/JOIN syntax
  • Take one full timed mock to fix per-section pacing
Common mistakes
  • Spending too long on one hard quant question and starving the rest of a timed section
  • Skipping the coding questions because MCQs feel safer
  • Ignoring English/verbal, which is easy marks many candidates leave on the table
What they look for
  • Consistent accuracy across all sections, not one spiked score
  • Clean, compiling code on the coding tasks
  • Finishing sections without excessive guessing
2

Technical + HR Interview

~30-45 min
CS fundamentals, projects, and behavioral/culture fit in one sitting

A single panel blends technical questioning with HR/behavioral questions. Technical depth is fundamentals-level: OOP, DBMS, one language, and your resume projects, not competitive DSA. The same panel then probes motivation, teamwork, relocation willingness and consulting-client readiness.

Example questions
  • Walk through a project on your resume and your specific contribution
  • Difference between two OOP concepts with a real example
  • Basic SQL query and normalization reasoning
  • Why Deloitte and why this service line
  • A time you handled a deadline or team conflict
How to prepare
  • Prepare a crisp 60-second story for every resume line and project
  • Revise OOP, DBMS and one language's basics to explain-out-loud level
  • Write STAR-format answers for 4-5 common behavioral prompts
  • Research Deloitte's service lines and recent India focus areas
Common mistakes
  • Listing projects you cannot explain in depth
  • Giving vague HR answers with no concrete example
  • Showing no awareness of what a consulting firm actually does
What they look for
  • Clear structured explanation of your own work
  • Honest 'I don't know' instead of bluffing
  • Genuine, specific reasons for wanting Deloitte

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude and data interpretation
  • Logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability and reading comprehension
  • OOP concepts
  • DBMS and SQL basics
  • One programming language (C/Java/Python)
  • Resume project depth
  • STAR behavioral storytelling

Eligibility

Typically 60% or 6.0 CGPA across 10th, 12th and graduation with no active backlogs; CSE/IT/ECE and allied branches for tech roles, broader streams for analyst roles. Confirm exact cutoffs per drive as they vary by campus and year (uncertain year-to-year).

Your prep plan

  • Days 1-3: quant and logical reasoning fundamentals with daily timed sets
  • Days 4-6: verbal ability plus C/Java output and SQL basics
  • Days 7-9: 40 easy-medium array/string coding problems
  • Days 10-11: revise OOP and DBMS to spoken-explanation level
  • Days 12-13: prepare project stories and 5 STAR behavioral answers
  • Day 14: one full timed mock assessment plus a mock interview
  • Take one timed full-length aptitude mock and review every miss
  • Rehearse every resume project out loud plus 'Why Deloitte'
  • Speed-revise OOP, DBMS and SQL cheat sheets

Frequently asked questions

Is coding heavily tested at Deloitte?

No. Expect only 1-2 easy coding problems in the online test and fundamentals-level technical questioning in the interview, not competitive DSA.

Is there still a Group Discussion round?

For most 2026 campus drives, no. The process now usually goes assessment straight to a combined technical-plus-HR interview, though some drives may still vary.

Is there negative marking?

Typically no negative marking in the online assessment, so attempt every question.

How technical is the interview really?

Fundamentals-focused: OOP, DBMS, SQL and your projects. It is not a LeetCode-hard round.

How fast is the process?

Often quick, with the interview shortly after the assessment and offers following within days to a couple of weeks.

What matters most in the HR portion?

Clear communication, genuine interest in Deloitte, and readiness for client-facing consulting work.
