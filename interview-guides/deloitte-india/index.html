Deloitte (India) Interview Guide (2026)

Runs a 4–5 stage funnel with a sectioned online assessment (including situational judgment) and multiple interviews up to partner level.

ModerateAssessment → interviews (3–5 weeks)Updated Sep 2026

The hiring process

An online assessment covering aptitude, technical MCQs, and situational judgment leads to a technical interview, an HR/behavioral round, and often a final partner/director interview.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online assessment~90 minQuant/logical/verbal aptitude, technical MCQs and code snippets, situational judgment
Technical interview~30–45 minCore subjects, projects, case-style problem solving
HR/behavioral round~20–30 minBehavioral questions, teamwork, communication
Partner/Director interview~20–30 minLeadership fit and alignment with values

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • Aptitude (quant, logical, verbal)
  • CS fundamentals and code snippets
  • Situational judgment/workplace scenarios
  • One language plus DSA basics
  • Project and resume depth
  • STAR-format behavioral answers

Eligibility

Commonly more than 60% in 10th, 12th, and graduation (full-time B.E./B.Tech/M.E./MCA). Fresher tracks include Business Technology Analyst and Associate Analyst Trainee. Varies by role and drive.

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–3: Aptitude across quant, logical, and verbal
  • Days 4–6: Technical MCQs, code snippets, and DSA basics
  • Days 7–8: Practice situational-judgment questions
  • Days 9–11: Prepare STAR-format behavioral stories and project talk
  • Days 12–14: Full mock assessment plus mock technical and behavioral rounds

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: Timed aptitude plus technical MCQs and situational-judgment practice
  • Day 2: Prepare 3–4 STAR stories and rehearse project explanation

FAQ

What is the situational judgment section?

It presents workplace scenarios and asks how you would respond. There is no code involved; answer in line with professionalism, teamwork, and client focus.

Is there really a partner round?

For many roles yes. A final partner/director interview checks culture fit and leadership potential rather than coding.

Which fresher profiles exist?

Reports cite Business Technology Analyst and Associate Analyst Trainee among the common engineering-fresher tracks, with content varying by service line.
