EY (India) Interview Guide (2026)

EY India hires freshers largely through EY GDS (Global Delivery Services) across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi and Kolkata for technology, assurance, tax and consulting tracks. The process is an online aptitude test followed by two to three rounds: a technical interview, an optional group exercise or case, and an HR interview.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Aptitude Assessment

~60-90 min
Quant, verbal, logical reasoning and sometimes a technical/coding MCQ block

A proctored MCQ test weighted toward data interpretation rather than raw arithmetic, with verbal and logical sections and sometimes a domain or programming MCQ block for tech roles. Typically no sectional cutoffs and no negative marking, so answer everything.

Example questions
  • Interpreting a data table to compute a ratio or growth
  • Logical sequences and syllogisms
  • Reading comprehension and para-jumbles
  • Basic programming or domain MCQs for the applied track
  • Numerical reasoning under time pressure
How to prepare
  • Practice data-interpretation sets daily on IndiaBix and PrepInsta free pages
  • Drill logical reasoning puzzles and syllogisms
  • Revise track-relevant fundamentals (CS basics for tech, accounting for assurance/tax)
  • Take one timed full mock to build speed
Common mistakes
  • Treating DI questions as pure arithmetic instead of reading the chart carefully
  • Leaving verbal marks on the table
  • Not tailoring prep to your applied service line
What they look for
  • Strong, balanced accuracy across sections
  • Speed on data interpretation
  • Correct track-relevant fundamentals
2

Group Exercise / Case (optional)

~20-30 min
Collaboration, structured thinking and communication

Some drives include a group discussion or short case exercise where you analyze a business scenario in a small group. Assessors watch how you contribute, structure a point, and build on others rather than dominate. Not every drive runs this round.

Example questions
  • A business or ethics scenario to discuss as a group
  • Structuring an approach to a client problem
  • Prioritizing options under a constraint
  • Summarizing the group's conclusion clearly
How to prepare
  • Practice speaking for 30-45 seconds with a clear point-reason-example structure
  • Read business and current-affairs summaries to build talking points
  • Practice inviting quieter members in to show collaboration
  • Learn a simple issue-tree framework for cases
Common mistakes
  • Dominating or interrupting to grab airtime
  • Staying silent the whole exercise
  • Making points with no structure or evidence
What they look for
  • Clear, concise contributions that move the discussion forward
  • Building on others' points
  • Calm, respectful tone
3

Technical Interview

~30-45 min
Track domain knowledge and resume depth

Content depends on your track: CS fundamentals and one language for technology roles, or finance/accounting concepts for assurance and tax. Expect resume-driven questions on your projects, internships and tools. Depth is fundamentals-solid, not competitive-DSA.

Example questions
  • Explain a project and your exact contribution
  • Core concept in your track (OOP/DBMS for tech, accounting standard for assurance)
  • A basic SQL query or a tool you listed
  • How you approached a problem in an internship
  • Trade-offs in a small design or process decision
How to prepare
  • Prepare deep, honest explanations of every resume item
  • Revise track fundamentals to spoken-explanation depth
  • For tech: OOP, DBMS, SQL and one language basics
  • For finance tracks: core accounting/audit concepts
Common mistakes
  • Overstating skills you can't defend
  • Memorized definitions with no examples
  • No clarity on your own project contribution
What they look for
  • Confident, specific project explanations
  • Correct fundamentals with real examples
  • Honesty about limits
4

HR Interview

~20-30 min
Communication, motivation and cultural fit

A conversational round assessing why EY, career goals, willingness to relocate or work in shifts, and general communication. This is where fit for a client-service culture is judged.

Example questions
  • Why EY and why this service line
  • Strengths, weaknesses and a growth area
  • A teamwork or conflict situation
  • Relocation and shift flexibility
  • Where you see yourself in a few years
How to prepare
  • Research EY's service lines and GDS model
  • Prepare 4-5 STAR behavioral stories
  • Rehearse a genuine 'Why EY' answer
  • Prepare honest answers on relocation and shifts
Common mistakes
  • Generic 'big brand' reasons for wanting EY
  • Rehearsed answers that sound fake
  • Hesitation on relocation when the role requires it
What they look for
  • Clear, warm communication
  • Specific, authentic motivation
  • Flexibility and maturity

What to master

  • Data interpretation and numerical reasoning
  • Logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability
  • OOP, DBMS and SQL (tech track)
  • Core accounting/audit concepts (assurance/tax track)
  • Resume project depth
  • Group-discussion and case structuring
  • STAR behavioral storytelling

Eligibility

Typically 60% or above in 10th, 12th and graduation with no active backlogs; CSE/IT/ECE/EEE for tech tracks and commerce/finance streams for assurance and tax. EY GDS is the main fresher-hiring entity in India. Confirm exact cutoffs per drive.

How the package is structured

  • GDS (Global Delivery Services) tracks pay slightly below client-facing.
  • Ratings drive bonus and promotion speed; up-or-out pressure exists.
  • Niche skills (SAP, cyber, data/AI) get premium offers.

Frequently asked questions

What is EY GDS?

EY Global Delivery Services is EY's captive delivery arm and the primary fresher-hiring entity in India, with major offices in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi and Kolkata.

Is there negative marking in the aptitude test?

Typically no negative marking and often no sectional cutoffs, so attempt every question.

Is the group exercise always included?

No. Some drives include a group discussion or case exercise, others skip straight to technical and HR. It varies by drive.

How technical is the tech-track interview?

Fundamentals-focused: OOP, DBMS, SQL, one language and your projects. It is not a competitive DSA round.

Do non-CS branches qualify?

Yes for many tracks; assurance and tax hire commerce/finance backgrounds, while tech tracks favor CSE/IT/ECE/EEE.

Does EY ask about shifts and relocation?

Yes, GDS roles can involve shifts and relocation, so be ready with honest answers.
