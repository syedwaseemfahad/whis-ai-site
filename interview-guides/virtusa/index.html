Virtusa Interview Guide (2026)

Virtusa hires freshers into Coder and Power Coder tracks (differing in package and coding depth) through a four-stage process: an online test with MCQ and coding sections, one to two technical interviews, an optional group discussion, and an HR round. The Power Coder path includes a harder advanced coding round.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Assessment

~90 min+
MCQ aptitude/technical plus one or two coding rounds

A ~60-question MCQ portion (quant, logical, technical, verbal, often 15 each, with -0.25 negative marking) followed by coding. There are commonly two coding rounds: an easy-medium set, and for Power Coder an advanced set. Negative marking means guessing MCQs costs you.

Example questions
  • Quant, logical, verbal and technical MCQs
  • Easy-medium coding (arrays, strings, math)
  • Advanced coding (DP, graphs) for Power Coder
  • Time-complexity awareness
  • Basic OOP/DBMS technical MCQs
How to prepare
  • Practice MCQ sets across quant/logical/verbal/technical
  • Solve easy-medium coding, plus DP/graphs for Power Coder
  • Note negative marking: only answer MCQs you're confident on
  • Take a timed full mock
Common mistakes
  • Guessing MCQs despite negative marking
  • Skipping the harder coding round for Power Coder
  • Partial solutions from missed edge cases
What they look for
  • Accurate MCQs without reckless guessing
  • Correct code across both rounds
  • Complexity awareness
2

Technical Interview(s)

~30-45 min each
OOP, SQL, DSA, collections and projects

One to two rounds on OOP in Java/Python, SQL queries, data structures, Java collections, and your resume projects. In-person rounds at the company probe fundamentals, collections, SQL and project depth.

Example questions
  • OOP concepts with examples
  • SQL query with joins/aggregation
  • Java collections and their trade-offs
  • A DSA problem or its approach
  • Project walkthrough and contribution
How to prepare
  • Revise OOP, collections and DBMS to spoken depth
  • Practice SQL queries
  • Solve DSA problems you can explain
  • Rehearse resume projects deeply
Common mistakes
  • Weak collections knowledge
  • SQL gaps
  • Unclear project ownership
What they look for
  • Solid OOP and collections
  • Correct SQL
  • Project ownership
3

Group Discussion (optional)

~15-25 min
Communication and teamwork

Some drives run a GD on a general or tech topic to assess communication, structure and collaboration. Not always included.

Example questions
  • A current-affairs or tech topic
  • Structuring and defending a viewpoint
  • Building on others' points
  • Summarizing the discussion
How to prepare
  • Practice concise, structured points
  • Read summaries of current tech topics
  • Practice inviting others in
  • Learn to open and close a GD
Common mistakes
  • Dominating or staying silent
  • Points without structure
  • Interrupting others
What they look for
  • Clear, structured contribution
  • Collaboration
  • Calm tone
4

HR Interview

~20-30 min
Cultural fit and career goals

A conversational round on motivation, cultural fit and career goals rather than technical depth. Candidates who clear the earlier rounds have a strong selection rate, so composure here matters.

Example questions
  • Why Virtusa and the Coder/Power Coder track
  • Career goals
  • A strength and a growth area
  • Relocation and joining readiness
  • A teamwork story
How to prepare
  • Research Virtusa and the two tracks
  • Prepare STAR behavioral stories
  • Rehearse an authentic 'Why Virtusa'
  • Prepare honest logistics answers
Common mistakes
  • Generic motivation
  • No behavioral examples
  • Uncertainty on logistics
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine fit
  • Logistical readiness

What to master

  • Quant, logical, verbal and technical MCQs
  • Easy-medium DSA
  • Advanced DSA (DP, graphs) for Power Coder
  • OOP and Java collections
  • SQL and DBMS
  • Time-complexity analysis
  • Resume project depth
  • STAR/GD communication

Eligibility

Typically 60% or 6.0+ CGPA across 10th, 12th and graduation. Coder (~5 LPA) and Power Coder (~6.5 LPA) tracks differ mainly by coding depth. Confirm exact cutoffs and packages per drive.

Your prep plan

  • Days 1-3: MCQ practice across all four sections (mind the negative marking)
  • Days 4-6: easy-medium coding; add DP/graphs if targeting Power Coder
  • Days 7-8: OOP, Java collections and DBMS revision
  • Days 9-10: SQL practice and DSA explanation
  • Days 11-12: rehearse resume projects and a GD
  • Days 13-14: timed mock test plus mock technical and HR
  • Timed MCQ-plus-coding mock (careful with guessing)
  • Revise OOP/collections/SQL cheat sheets
  • Rehearse projects plus 'Why Virtusa'

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Coder and Power Coder?

Power Coder targets a higher package (~6.5 LPA vs ~5 LPA) and includes a harder advanced coding round; both share the same overall funnel.

Is there negative marking?

Yes, the MCQ portion commonly has -0.25 per wrong answer, so only answer questions you're confident about.

How many coding rounds are there?

Often two: an easy-medium set and, for Power Coder, an advanced set with DP/graph-level problems.

Is the group discussion always held?

No, the GD is optional and depends on the drive.

How technical is the interview?

OOP, Java collections, SQL, DSA and projects. Fundamentals-solid, with more coding depth expected for Power Coder.

What matters in HR?

Communication, cultural fit and career goals rather than technical depth.
