Infosys Interview Guide (2026)

Infosys hires freshers through a no-negative-marking online aptitude test followed by technical and HR interviews, with parallel higher tracks (Specialist Programmer and Power Programmer) that demand serious coding. What sets Infosys apart is the weight it gives to InfyTQ and HackWithInfy performance, which can fast-track you into the better-paid programmer roles.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Aptitude Test

~95 min
Reasoning, quant, and verbal screening

Around 65 questions across quantitative, logical reasoning, and verbal ability with no negative marking. Sections are timed and act as the primary filter. For programmer tracks, a pseudocode or coding component is added on top.

Example questions
  • Data interpretation and arithmetic word problems
  • Logical puzzles: syllogisms, arrangements, coding-decoding
  • Verbal: reading comprehension and error spotting
  • Pseudocode output-tracing (for programmer tracks)
  • Number series and pattern completion
How to prepare
  • Practice free sectional sets and take timed mocks
  • For programmer roles, drill pseudocode reading on GeeksforGeeks
  • Build mental-math speed for the quant section
  • Revise grammar and practice RC skimming techniques
Common mistakes
  • Under-preparing verbal, which many technical students neglect
  • Poor time budgeting across sections
  • Skipping pseudocode practice for programmer tracks
What they look for
  • Balanced accuracy across all sections
  • Speed under time pressure
  • Logical reasoning strength
2

Technical Interview

20-45 min
CS fundamentals, coding, and projects

The System Engineer track usually has one to two interview rounds; the higher DSE/programmer tracks have more, with a stronger coding component. Expect resume-driven questions, core CS subjects, and simple problem-solving; harder DSA appears for programmer roles.

Example questions
  • Explain a project and the tech choices you made
  • OOP principles with real examples from your code
  • Write or dry-run a small algorithm (string/array)
  • DBMS query writing and normalization
  • OS or networking basics depending on your resume
How to prepare
  • Deeply revise one language plus OOP, DBMS, and OS
  • Solve easy-medium DSA if targeting programmer tracks
  • Prepare clear, honest project walkthroughs
  • Do a mock technical interview with follow-up questions
Common mistakes
  • Claiming skills you cannot demonstrate
  • Weak project ownership when questioned deeply
  • For programmer roles, being unable to write clean code live
What they look for
  • Strong fundamentals over jargon
  • Genuine project depth
  • Clear coding logic (especially for programmer roles)
3

HR Interview

10-20 min
Communication, motivation, and fit

Assesses communication, cultural fit, flexibility on location and shift, and joining logistics. Generally not heavily eliminatory unless communication is poor or answers are inconsistent.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself and your strengths/weaknesses
  • Why Infosys and why this role
  • Willingness to relocate and work across domains
  • How you handle pressure or feedback
  • Long-term career goals
How to prepare
  • Prepare a concise personal pitch
  • Research Infosys's business and recent initiatives
  • Have honest, consistent answers on relocation and flexibility
  • Keep documents ready
Common mistakes
  • Generic answers with no company knowledge
  • Inconsistency between technical and HR answers
  • Appearing inflexible on standard terms
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Adaptability and low attrition risk

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude and DI
  • Logical reasoning and puzzles
  • Verbal ability and grammar
  • Pseudocode and one programming language
  • OOP concepts
  • DBMS fundamentals and SQL
  • Operating systems basics
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

Minimum 60% or 6.0 CGPA in Class 10, Class 12, and graduation; strictly no active backlogs at application. Higher tracks like Power Programmer typically expect 7.5+ CGPA plus strong HackWithInfy/InfyTQ performance. Exact criteria vary by drive.

How the package is structured

Frequently asked questions

Is InfyTQ certification mandatory?

It is not strictly mandatory, but certified candidates are often preferred, shortlisted faster (especially off-campus), and better positioned for the programmer tracks.

What is the difference between System Engineer and Specialist/Power Programmer?

System Engineer is the standard fresher role. Specialist and Power Programmer are higher-paid tracks with a much harder coding bar, often gated by HackWithInfy or InfyTQ performance and higher CGPA.

Is there negative marking in the online test?

As reported, Infosys does not use negative marking, so attempt everything within the section time limits.

How many interview rounds are there?

The System Engineer track usually has one to two rounds combining technical and HR. Programmer tracks tend to have more rounds with deeper coding.

Do backlogs matter?

Yes, you generally need no active backlogs at the time of applying. Confirm the exact rule for your specific drive.

How coding-heavy is the standard track?

For System Engineer, expect basic-to-easy problem solving and pseudocode. Serious DSA is mainly for the programmer tracks.
