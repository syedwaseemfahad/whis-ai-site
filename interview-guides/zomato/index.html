Zomato Interview Guide (2026)

Zomato's SWE loop runs 4-5 rounds over 2-3 weeks and is known for architectural thinking even at SDE-1: an OA, a DSA-plus-projects round, a system design round (HLD and LLD) with OS/DB/networking fundamentals, a decisive managerial culture-fit round, and HR. It rewards candidates who proactively discuss scalability, caching, sharding, queues, and resilience.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Coding Assessment

~90-120 min
DSA + debugging filter

Usually on HackerRank or an internal platform, with 2-3 medium-to-hard DSA problems plus some debugging tasks. Clean, correct solutions advance you.

Example questions
  • Medium-to-hard array/string problems
  • Graph or tree traversal
  • DP or greedy
  • Debugging a provided snippet
  • Hash-map and sorting logic
How to prepare
  • Drill medium-hard DSA (NeetCode, free)
  • Practice debugging unfamiliar code
  • Do timed multi-problem sets
  • Review complexity and edge cases
Common mistakes
  • Underestimating difficulty
  • Ignoring the debugging component
  • Poor time management
What they look for
  • Correct solutions to harder problems
  • Clean debugging
  • Efficient time use
2

Technical Round 1 (DSA + Projects)

~60-80 min
Live coding and project depth

A live coding round on DSA plus a deep dive into your resume projects and the technical decisions behind them. Interviewers probe both your algorithmic skill and engineering judgment.

Example questions
  • Graph or tree problem
  • Sliding window / two pointers
  • Heap or stack based problem
  • Deep dive on a project's architecture
  • Trade-offs you made in past work
How to prepare
  • Drill live-coding mediums with narration
  • Prepare a detailed project deep-dive
  • Be ready to justify technical decisions
  • Rehearse complexity discussion
Common mistakes
  • Shallow project answers that fold under questioning
  • Silent coding
  • No complexity discussion
What they look for
  • Strong coding plus engineering judgment
  • Deep project ownership
  • Clear reasoning
3

Technical Round 2 (System Design)

~60 min
HLD/LLD + CS fundamentals

A design round covering both high-level and low-level design plus fundamentals across OS, databases, and networking. Zomato expects real-world architectural thinking even at SDE-1.

Example questions
  • Design order/food-delivery flow with scaling
  • Caching strategy and DB sharding
  • Message queue design and circuit breakers
  • LLD of a service (cart, restaurant catalog)
  • OS/DB/networking fundamentals
How to prepare
  • Study system-design-primer + LLD/SOLID (free)
  • Learn caching, sharding, queues, circuit breakers
  • Revise OS/DBMS/networking
  • Do 2 mock designs discussing resilience
Common mistakes
  • Not proactively discussing scalability
  • Weak on fundamentals when probed
  • Ignoring failure handling
What they look for
  • Proactive scalability and resilience thinking
  • Solid CS fundamentals
  • Clear HLD and LLD
4

Managerial / Culture Fit

~45-60 min
Ownership and judgment (decisive)

Often with a senior engineer or lead from another team, this is the round candidates most consistently call decisive. Expect ownership, ambiguity, and judgment questions alongside project discussion.

Example questions
  • A time you took ownership beyond your scope
  • Handling ambiguity or conflicting priorities
  • A production issue and your response
  • Disagreement with a teammate or lead
  • Why Zomato
How to prepare
  • Prepare 4-5 strong STAR stories
  • Emphasize ownership and impact
  • Practice honest, self-aware answers
  • Research Zomato's business and scale
Common mistakes
  • Vague, low-ownership stories
  • No specific motivation
  • Underestimating this round
What they look for
  • Strong ownership mindset
  • Sound judgment under ambiguity
  • Genuine interest
5

HR / Offer

~20-30 min
Logistics and fit

A shorter conversation covering compensation, timelines, and next steps.

Example questions
  • Compensation expectations
  • Notice period and availability
  • Motivation and goals
  • Location preferences
  • General culture fit
How to prepare
  • Know your comp range
  • Be clear on logistics
  • Have a concise 'why Zomato'
  • Stay professional
Common mistakes
  • Unprepared on comp
  • Inconsistent motivation
  • Negativity about past roles
What they look for
  • Reasonable expectations
  • Consistent motivation
  • Professionalism

What to master

  • Arrays, strings, graphs, trees, heaps
  • DP, greedy, sliding window
  • Debugging skills
  • HLD: caching, sharding, queues, circuit breakers
  • LLD/SOLID
  • OS, DBMS, networking fundamentals
  • Ownership and STAR stories
  • Scalability and resilience thinking

Eligibility

SDE-1 and above; architectural thinking is expected even at SDE-1, with deeper HLD for higher levels.

Your prep plan

  • Days 1-3: Medium-hard DSA + debugging practice
  • Days 4-5: Live-coding + project deep-dive prep
  • Days 6-9: HLD (delivery flow) + caching/sharding/queues
  • Days 10-11: LLD + OS/DB/networking revision
  • Days 12-13: Ownership STAR stories
  • Day 14: Full mock loop
  • Drill 12 medium-hard problems + one debugging exercise
  • One HLD template (delivery flow) with caching/sharding/queues
  • Prepare 3 ownership STAR stories + revise CS fundamentals

Frequently asked questions

Is system design expected at SDE-1?

Yes. Zomato emphasizes architectural thinking even at SDE-1; you should proactively discuss scalability, caching, and resilience.

What scalability topics should I know?

Caching strategy, database sharding, message queue design, and circuit breakers for dependent-service failures are specifically rewarded.

How important are CS fundamentals?

Fairly. The design round often touches OS, databases, and networking, so revise them.

Which round is decisive?

Candidates most consistently describe the managerial/culture-fit round as the decisive one. Prepare strong ownership stories.

Does the OA include debugging?

Often yes; alongside DSA problems there can be debugging tasks, so practice reading and fixing code.

How long is the process?

Roughly 2-3 weeks across 4-5 rounds.
