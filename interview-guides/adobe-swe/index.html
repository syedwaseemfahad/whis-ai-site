The hiring process

An online assessment (HackerRank/CodeSignal, sometimes with CS-fundamentals MCQs) leads into 2–3 DSA coding rounds, a system design round (HLD + LLD for mid/senior), and a hiring-manager or director behavioral close; roles are titled Computer Scientist / MTS in India.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online assessment~60–90 min2–3 DSA problems, sometimes CS MCQs (OS, DBMS, networks)
Coding (x2–3)~60 min eachDSA in C++/Java, clean code, complexity
System design~60 minHLD + LLD for mid/senior; design choices and trade-offs
Hiring manager~45 minbehavioral, projects, product interest

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • arrays, strings, hashing
  • trees, graphs, recursion
  • dynamic programming
  • OS/DBMS/networks fundamentals
  • system design (HLD + LLD)
  • language depth in C++/Java

Eligibility

open — Computer Scientist 1/2 or MTS levels in India; not CGPA-gated

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–5: DSA mediums in C++/Java with strong complexity discipline
  • Days 6–8: brush up OS, DBMS and networking fundamentals for the OA/MCQs
  • Days 9–11: practice HLD + LLD, explaining design choices out loud
  • Days 12–13: mock loop of 2 coding + 1 design
  • Day 14: prep hiring-manager stories and revisit weak topics

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: 10 DSA mediums plus a fundamentals refresher
  • Day 2: one HLD/LLD walkthrough and 4 behavioral stories

FAQ

Which language should I use?

C++ or Java are the common choices; interviewers value clean code and clear reasoning about design decisions.

Does the OA test CS theory?

Often yes. Adobe assessments frequently mix DSA with MCQs on OS, DBMS and networks, so review fundamentals alongside coding.

How much design is there?

Mid and senior candidates get a real design round covering both high-level and low-level design, weighing alternatives matters more than one perfect answer.
