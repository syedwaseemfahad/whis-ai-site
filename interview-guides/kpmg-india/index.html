KPMG (India) Interview Guide (2026)

Runs a 4–6 stage process; engineering freshers usually enter Advisory's tech/analytics practice via an aptitude test then technical and HR interviews.

ModerateAssessment → interviews (3–5 weeks)Updated Sep 2026

The hiring process

An online aptitude assessment leads to an optional group discussion or case, then a technical interview on core CS and an HR interview on client-facing communication. Stage count varies by service line.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online assessment~60–90 minNumerical, verbal, and logical reasoning
Group discussion / case (some drives)~15–20 minCommunication and structured thinking
Technical interview~30–45 minCore CS concepts and projects
HR interview~20–30 minCommunication under client-facing pressure and fit

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • Aptitude (numerical, verbal, logical)
  • Core CS fundamentals
  • One language basics
  • Analytics/data basics for GTA roles
  • Communication and GD practice
  • STAR behavioral answers

Eligibility

Commonly minimum 60% aggregate or equivalent CGPA with no active backlogs at joining; B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech/MCA in CS/IT/ECE for tech roles, though thresholds vary by business unit and drive.

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–3: Aptitude practice across numerical, verbal, and logical
  • Days 4–6: Core CS revision
  • Days 7–8: Analytics/data basics if targeting GTA
  • Days 9–11: GD practice and STAR stories
  • Days 12–14: Full mock assessment plus mock technical and HR

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: Timed aptitude plus core CS revision
  • Day 2: One GD topic, STAR stories, and a mock interview

FAQ

Which practice do engineering freshers join?

Reports say campus engineering freshers most often enter Advisory's Global Technology and Analytics (GTA) practice.

Do non-engineering grads have a different path?

Yes. B.Com and BBA graduates typically enter Audit and Tax tracks with different interview content from the tech track.

How many rounds should I expect?

Roughly 4–6 depending on role and program; the exact count and whether a GD or case is included varies by service line and drive.
