KPMG (India) Interview Guide (2026)

KPMG India hires freshers for advisory, technology, audit and tax roles, usually through an online aptitude test followed by a technical round and an HR round, often on the same campus day. Some drives add a group discussion or short case presentation. The technical bar is a fundamentals check, not a competitive coding marathon.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Aptitude Assessment

~45-60 min
Quant, logical reasoning and verbal under time pressure

An MCQ test covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning and often a short verbal section. The aim is speed-with-accuracy rather than trick math. Some technology drives add a short CS-fundamentals or coding block.

Example questions
  • Percentages, ratios and simple data interpretation
  • Logical puzzles and seating/arrangement problems
  • Sentence correction and comprehension
  • Number series and pattern completion
  • Short CS-fundamentals MCQs for tech roles
How to prepare
  • Practice timed quant and logical sets on IndiaBix/PrepInsta
  • Drill verbal ability and comprehension
  • For tech roles, revise OOP/DBMS MCQs
  • Take one full timed mock for pacing
Common mistakes
  • Losing time on one hard puzzle
  • Neglecting verbal easy marks
  • Not confirming whether your drive has a coding block
What they look for
  • Balanced accuracy and speed
  • Clean handling of reasoning puzzles
  • Correct fundamentals on tech MCQs
2

Technical Interview

~30-40 min
CS/domain fundamentals and resume projects

For tech roles the panel checks Java/OOP, DBMS theory, data-structure definitions, OS concepts and cloud basics, plus your projects. Advisory and audit tracks probe domain fundamentals. It is a fundamentals check, so clear, correct basics beat memorized edge cases.

Example questions
  • Explain OOP pillars with a real example
  • DBMS keys, normalization and a simple SQL query
  • Data structure use-cases and time complexity basics
  • A project walkthrough and your contribution
  • Basic cloud or OS concept you have listed
How to prepare
  • Revise OOP, DBMS, OS and DSA definitions to explain-out-loud level
  • Prepare SQL query practice
  • Rehearse every resume project deeply
  • Skim cloud fundamentals if you listed cloud skills
Common mistakes
  • Bluffing on unfamiliar topics
  • Reciting definitions without examples
  • Weak grasp of your own projects
What they look for
  • Clear, correct fundamentals
  • Good examples tied to concepts
  • Honest scope of knowledge
3

HR Interview

~20-30 min
Communication, motivation and consulting fit

A conversational round assessing communication clarity, a genuine two-year plan, one real growth area, and comfort with client-facing work. Composure and professionalism matter as much as the answers.

Example questions
  • Why KPMG and why this practice
  • A genuine strength and a real weakness
  • A client-facing or teamwork situation
  • Your two-year career plan
  • Handling pressure or ambiguity
How to prepare
  • Research KPMG's advisory and audit practices
  • Prepare STAR stories for teamwork and pressure
  • Rehearse an authentic 'Why KPMG'
  • Have an honest growth-area answer ready
Common mistakes
  • Cliched motivation answers
  • Fake or evasive weakness answers
  • Low energy or poor communication
What they look for
  • Clear, confident communication
  • Authentic motivation and self-awareness
  • Client-ready professionalism

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude
  • Logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability
  • OOP, DBMS and SQL
  • Data structures and OS basics
  • Cloud fundamentals (if listed)
  • Resume project depth
  • STAR behavioral storytelling

Eligibility

Typically 60% or 6.0 CGPA across 10th, 12th and graduation with no active backlogs; CSE/IT for technology roles and commerce/finance for audit and tax. Confirm exact cutoffs per drive.

Frequently asked questions

Is KPMG's technical round competitive DSA?

No. It is a fundamentals check: OOP, DBMS theory, data-structure definitions, OS and cloud basics, plus your projects.

Do technical and HR happen the same day?

At most campuses, yes, and clearing both often leads to an offer within about a week.

Is there always a group discussion?

No. Some drives add a GD or short case presentation, depending on role and business unit.

Do non-CS candidates qualify?

Yes for audit, tax and advisory tracks; technology roles favor CSE/IT.

What do they most want in the HR round?

Clear communication, a genuine two-year plan, one real growth area, and comfort with client-facing work.

Is coding in the online test?

Some technology drives add a short CS-fundamentals or coding block, but many drives are aptitude-only. Confirm per drive.
