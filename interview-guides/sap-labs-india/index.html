SAP Labs India Interview Guide (2026)

Product-company process (e.g. Scholar@SAP) with an aptitude/coding test, two technical rounds, a managerial discussion, and an HR screen.

The hiring process

An online assessment mixing aptitude and 1–2 coding problems leads to two technical interviews (heavy on Java/C++ and SQL), a managerial discussion, and an HR screen.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online assessment~90–120 minMaths, reasoning, verbal, CS fundamentals, 2 coding problems
Technical interview 1~45 minJava/C++, DSA, SQL queries
Technical interview 2~45 minDeeper coding, problem solving, projects
Managerial discussion~30 minDesign/scenario reasoning and approach
HR screening~20–30 minCommunication and fit

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • Medium-level DSA and coding
  • Java and C++ depth
  • SQL and DBMS
  • OS/CS fundamentals
  • Problem decomposition
  • Project depth

Eligibility

Commonly BE/B.Tech/ME/M.Tech/MCA with 60% or above in 10th, 12th, and graduation, no active backlogs at application, and a maximum education gap of about one year. Varies by drive.

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–3: Aptitude and reasoning practice
  • Days 4–7: Daily medium-level DSA and Java/C++ coding
  • Days 8–10: SQL, DBMS, and OS revision
  • Days 11–12: Design/scenario reasoning and project prep
  • Days 13–14: Two full mock technical rounds plus a mock managerial round

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: 10–12 coding problems (Java/C++) plus SQL practice
  • Day 2: DBMS/OS revision, project prep, and a mock technical interview

FAQ

How many interview rounds are there?

Reports describe up to five stages: the online test, two technical interviews, a managerial discussion, and an HR screen.

Which languages should I focus on?

Java and C++ are tested most, and SQL queries are standard in the first technical round.

Can off-campus candidates apply?

Yes. SAP runs on-campus drives across many colleges, and students from non-listed colleges can apply for off-campus roles via the SAP Careers portal.
