ICICI Bank Interview Guide (2026)

ICICI Bank operates heavily on digital banking, mobile and API platforms, and data systems, so its technology and graduate-engineer hiring seeks practical programming skills, database competence, and reliability-minded thinking. Interviews favor applied CS, SQL, and clear communication over hard algorithms. Given the regulated setting, panels also weigh integrity, adaptability, and long-term commitment.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Aptitude + Technical Test

~60–90 min
Aptitude, CS, banking awareness

An online test combining quantitative and logical aptitude, technical MCQs, and sometimes English or banking-awareness sections, with occasional basic coding. The focus is broad screening rather than deep algorithms. Sectional cutoffs may apply in some drives.

Example questions
  • Quantitative aptitude and data interpretation
  • Logical reasoning and verbal ability
  • Programming, DBMS, and networking MCQs
  • Basic SQL and output-prediction
  • General and banking awareness
How to prepare
  • Practice aptitude, DI, and reasoning (free: IndiaBix)
  • Revise CS-fundamentals MCQs across DBMS, OS, networking (free: GeeksforGeeks)
  • Brush up SQL basics and simple coding
  • Skim current banking and general awareness
Common mistakes
  • Ignoring sectional cutoffs and failing one weak area
  • Skipping banking-awareness prep when a drive includes it
What they look for
  • Balanced scores across all sections
  • Solid CS fundamentals and SQL basics
2

Technical Interview

~30–45 min
Applied CS, SQL, projects

A discussion of programming fundamentals, databases, and your projects, framed around practical digital-banking systems. Expect SQL queries, OOP concepts, and scenario questions on data handling or building a simple feature. Interest in APIs and digital platforms is viewed favorably.

Example questions
  • SQL query writing and optimization
  • OOP and core language fundamentals
  • Practical data-structure use
  • Designing or reasoning about a simple API or feature
  • Project deep-dive with a reliability lens
How to prepare
  • Practice SQL joins, grouping, and subqueries
  • Revise OOP and your main programming language
  • Prepare a practical, reliability-aware project story
  • Learn basics of digital-banking and API-driven systems
Common mistakes
  • Weak SQL despite its central role
  • Projects explained with no practical or reliability framing
What they look for
  • Strong SQL and applied CS reasoning
  • Awareness of digital-banking technology
3

HR / Behavioral (and sometimes GD)

~20–40 min
Fit, integrity, communication

An HR conversation on motivation, goals, stability, and salary, with situational and ethics questions suited to banking; some drives include a group discussion on a business or current-affairs topic. Communication, integrity, and adaptability are the focus.

Example questions
  • Why ICICI Bank and banking technology
  • Long-term commitment and relocation openness
  • Handling an ethical or compliance situation
  • Group-discussion reasoning on a topical issue
How to prepare
  • Prepare a genuine why-ICICI and why-banking-tech answer
  • Practice structured points for a possible group discussion
  • Prepare one integrity and one adaptability story
  • Research ICICI's digital-banking initiatives
Common mistakes
  • Dominating or staying silent in a group discussion
  • Treating the role as a temporary stopgap
What they look for
  • Clear communication and balanced GD participation
  • Integrity and long-term intent

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude and data interpretation
  • Logical reasoning and verbal ability
  • Programming fundamentals and OOP
  • DBMS and SQL
  • Operating systems and networking basics
  • APIs and digital-banking concepts
  • Software reliability basics
  • Banking and general awareness

Eligibility

Role-based: technology graduate-engineer and IT roles generally require a CS/IT engineering degree or MCA with coding and DBMS fundamentals; program-specific criteria (batch year, marks, bonds for some trainee tracks) vary by drive and posting.

How the package is structured

  • Tech track pays below product firms with no equity; comp is dominated by fixed pay plus a small performance bonus.
  • Front-office/relationship-banking roles earn more than tech at comparable grades.
  • Digital-banking, data-science, and core-platform teams command the higher end of internal ranges.
  • Moving to a fintech (Navi, Dream11) or product GCC is the fastest comp accelerator.

Frequently asked questions

Are there sectional cutoffs in ICICI's test?

Some drives apply them, so a weak area like aptitude or verbal can eliminate you even with strong technicals; prepare all sections.

Is there a group discussion?

It depends on the drive. Several tech and trainee tracks include a GD, so practicing structured, balanced participation helps.

How technical is the interview?

Moderately. It emphasizes SQL, DBMS, OOP, and practical project reasoning over hard algorithms, with bonus points for digital-banking awareness.
