The hiring process

Typically four eliminatory rounds, a DSA coding assessment/interview, a low-level design round, a high-level system design round, and a hiring-manager round, with interviewers probing CS fundamentals and SaaS product sense throughout.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Coding / DSA~60–90 min3 medium/hard problems (often a home assessment first)
Low-level design~60 minOOP modeling, clean extensible classes
High-level design~60 minHLD: DB internals, APIs, rate limiting, trade-offs
Hiring manager~45 minbehavioral, project architecture, product sense

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • arrays, strings, hashing, DSA mediums
  • low-level / OOP design
  • high-level design (rate limiting, APIs)
  • database internals
  • OS/networking fundamentals
  • behavioral and product sense

Eligibility

open — SDE through lead levels; not CGPA-gated

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–5: DSA mediums/hards for the coding assessment
  • Days 6–8: LLD practice with clean, extensible OOP
  • Days 9–11: HLD fundamentals (APIs, rate limiting, DB internals, caching)
  • Days 12–13: mock loop of 1 DSA + 1 design and prepare project stories
  • Day 14: review CS fundamentals and trade-off reasoning

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: 10 DSA mediums plus one LLD design
  • Day 2: one HLD walkthrough (e.g. rate limiter) and behavioral prep

FAQ

How many rounds are there?

Usually four eliminatory rounds, DSA, LLD, HLD and hiring manager, though some candidates see a compressed three-round version.

What do the design rounds reward?

Reasoning about alternatives and trade-offs, not just one working answer. Design internals and API principles come up often.

Does product sense matter?

Yes. Since you would build a live SaaS product, interviewers probe CS fundamentals and how you think about real customers.
