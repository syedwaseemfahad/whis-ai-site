Swiggy Interview Guide (2026)

A DSA-strong loop where low-level design is the real filter.

The hiring process

An online coding assessment (3 problems in ~60–90 min for DSA roles) leads into 2–3 technical rounds mixing DSA and low-level design, then a bar-raiser/managerial round and an HR conversation; LLD consistently trips up strong coders.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online assessment~60–90 min3 medium DSA problems on HackerRank
Technical 1 (DSA)~60 min2 mediums (arrays, intervals) plus language basics
Technical 2 (LLD)~60 minlow-level/OOP design, the key filter
Bar-raiser / HM~45–60 minsystem design and ownership; hiring-manager round
HR~30 minfit and compensation

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • arrays, intervals, hashing
  • sliding window and recursion
  • low-level design (OOP)
  • system design for senior roles
  • Java/backend fundamentals
  • behavioral / ownership

Eligibility

open — SDE-1 through SDE-2+; not CGPA-gated

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–5: DSA mediums (arrays, intervals, sliding window, hashing)
  • Days 6–9: dedicated low-level design practice (this is where many candidates fail)
  • Days 10–12: system design for senior roles plus Java/backend fundamentals
  • Day 13: mock loop of 1 DSA + 1 LLD
  • Day 14: ownership stories and complexity review

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: 10 DSA mediums plus one LLD design
  • Day 2: one system design walkthrough and behavioral prep

FAQ

What is the hardest round?

Low-level design. Candidates who ace DSA still get rejected here, so treat LLD as a first-class prep area.

What does the OA look like?

Usually three medium DSA problems in 60–90 minutes on HackerRank; full-stack roles may instead build a CRUD app over ~3 hours.

How long is the whole process?

Around 24 days on average, though it can range from 2 to 6 weeks depending on scheduling.
