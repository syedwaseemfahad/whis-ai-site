Amdocs Interview Guide (2026)

Hires SDEs via a multi-section online test (aptitude, technical, coding) followed by technical and HR interviews.

Easy–ModerateOnline test → interviews (2–4 weeks)Updated Sep 2026

The hiring process

A written/online test with aptitude, English, technical, and coding sections shortlists candidates for a technical interview and an HR round.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online test~90–120 minQuant, English, technical (Unix/SQL/OS/C outputs), 2–3 coding problems
Technical interview~30–45 minDBMS, data structures (trees/traversal), language concepts, projects
HR interview~15–30 minPersonality, communication, strengths/weaknesses, fit

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • Quant and verbal aptitude
  • SQL/PL-SQL and Unix basics
  • OS and DBMS
  • Data structures (trees, traversal)
  • C output/prediction questions
  • Basic coding and projects

Eligibility

Specific CGPA/percentage cutoffs vary by drive; strong academics, no backlogs, and good communication help. Confirm each drive's notification.

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–3: Aptitude and English practice
  • Days 4–6: SQL/PL-SQL, Unix, and C-output questions
  • Days 7–9: OS and DBMS plus data-structure basics
  • Days 10–12: Coding practice and project prep
  • Days 13–14: Full mock test plus mock technical and HR

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: Timed aptitude plus SQL/Unix/C-output practice
  • Day 2: Data-structure and DBMS revision, coding, and a mock interview

FAQ

What technical topics come up in the test?

Reports mention Unix, SQL/PL-SQL, operating systems, and C output-prediction questions alongside the coding section.

How many coding problems are there?

Typically 2–3 in the online test, at a basic-to-moderate level.

What does the technical interview focus on?

DBMS, data structures (including trees and traversal), language concepts, and your projects.
