Wipro Interview Guide (2026)

Wipro hires freshers through its National Level Talent Hunt (NLTH), a short aptitude test paired with a mandatory essay and a coding section for engineering graduates, followed by technical and HR interviews. The written essay is a distinctive step: your written English is actually scored, not just your MCQs, and top coders can be routed to premium Turbo/Elite tracks.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

NLTH Online Aptitude + Essay

~60 min
Aptitude and written English

A compact aptitude block (quant, logical reasoning, verbal) plus a written essay on a given topic, with no negative marking. The essay is genuinely evaluated for structure, grammar, and coherence, so it is not a throwaway.

Example questions
  • Quantitative: percentages, profit-loss, time-and-work
  • Logical reasoning: series, coding-decoding, arrangements
  • Verbal: comprehension and sentence correction
  • Essay on a general or opinion topic in a fixed word range
  • Data interpretation basics
How to prepare
  • Practice a 200-300 word structured essay in 15-20 minutes
  • Drill timed aptitude sections on free platforms
  • Revise grammar so the essay reads cleanly
  • Take full NLTH-style mocks including the essay
Common mistakes
  • Treating the essay casually or leaving it incomplete
  • Grammatical errors and no clear intro-body-conclusion in the essay
  • Poor time management between MCQs and essay
What they look for
  • Structured, grammatically clean writing
  • Balanced aptitude accuracy
  • Ability to organize thoughts quickly
2

Coding Test

45-60 min
Hands-on programming

Engineering graduates across branches face 2-3 coding problems. Data structures and simple dynamic programming are the most-tested areas. Strong performance can push you toward the Turbo/Elite premium track.

Example questions
  • Array or string manipulation with edge cases
  • A problem using a stack, queue, or hash map
  • Simple recursion or a basic DP problem
  • Number-theory or math-based logic problem
  • Sorting/searching-based question
How to prepare
  • Solve 30-40 easy-medium problems on HackerRank/LeetCode
  • Practice input parsing and exact output formatting
  • Drill basic DSA: arrays, strings, stacks, hashing, simple DP
  • Time yourself to finish at least one problem fully
Common mistakes
  • Passing samples but failing hidden edge cases
  • Not attempting the harder problem at all
  • Poor time allocation across problems
What they look for
  • Correct, test-case-passing solutions
  • Clean edge-case handling
  • Coding speed and clarity
3

Technical Interview

20-40 min
CS fundamentals and coding depth

Covers coding, DBMS, OS, and OOP fundamentals along with your resume projects. Depth varies by track; premium-track candidates get harder questions.

Example questions
  • Explain and defend a project decision
  • OOP concepts with examples
  • SQL query writing and DBMS basics
  • OS concepts (process, thread, deadlock at a basic level)
  • Dry-run or explain a coding solution
How to prepare
  • Revise DBMS, OS, OOP, and one language thoroughly
  • Prepare concise project walkthroughs
  • Practice explaining code aloud
  • Do a mock technical interview
Common mistakes
  • Overstating resume skills
  • Weak project ownership
  • Being unable to write basic SQL
What they look for
  • Solid fundamentals
  • Genuine project understanding
  • Clear technical communication
4

HR Interview

10-20 min
Behavioral fit and logistics

Assesses motivation, relocation willingness, team fit, and salary/joining expectations, plus acceptance of the standard service agreement.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why Wipro and why IT services
  • Relocation and shift flexibility
  • Strengths, weaknesses, and goals
  • Comfort with the service bond
How to prepare
  • Prepare a crisp self-introduction
  • Research Wipro's services and values
  • Have consistent answers on relocation and the bond
  • Keep documents organized
Common mistakes
  • Hesitating on the standard service agreement
  • Generic, unresearched answers
  • Inconsistency across rounds
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Flexibility and low attrition risk

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude
  • Logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability and essay writing
  • Core DSA and one language
  • OOP concepts
  • DBMS and SQL
  • Operating systems basics
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech (typically any branch); minimum 60% independently in 10th, 12th, and degree; no active backlogs, though cleared backlogs are usually acceptable. Exact criteria vary by drive.

Your prep plan

  • Days 1-3: Aptitude diagnostic and weak-topic rebuild; start essay practice
  • Days 4-6: Reasoning, verbal, and daily timed essays
  • Days 7-9: Code 30+ easy-medium DSA problems
  • Days 10-11: Full NLTH mocks including essay and coding
  • Days 12-13: Core CS revision and project prep
  • Day 14: Mock technical and HR interviews
  • Day 1: Aptitude mock plus two timed essays; revise DBMS/OOP
  • Day 2 morning: Solve 6-8 DSA problems with correct I/O
  • Day 2 afternoon: Prepare projects and rehearse HR answers

Frequently asked questions

Does the essay actually matter?

Yes. The NLTH essay is scored for structure, grammar, and coherence, so treat it as a real evaluated component, not filler.

What are the salary tracks?

Wipro hires through a standard track and higher Elite/Turbo tracks. Strong coding performance can route you into the premium track with better pay.

Is there negative marking?

The NLTH aptitude test is reported to have no negative marking, so attempt every question.

Are cleared backlogs a problem?

Active backlogs typically disqualify you, but cleared backlogs are usually acceptable. Confirm per drive.

How hard is the coding round?

Expect 2-3 problems at easy-to-medium level, with data structures and simple DP most common. Finishing at least one problem cleanly is important.

Is there a service agreement?

Wipro fresher offers commonly include a standard service agreement. Be ready to discuss and accept it in the HR round.
