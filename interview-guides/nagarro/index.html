Nagarro Interview Guide (2026)

Nagarro runs one of the more coding-intensive fresher processes among IT firms: two written tests (general aptitude, a technical ability test, and a coding test with three problems), followed by a technical interview and an HR round. Its coding round is genuinely demanding, mixing easy, medium, and hard problems, so strong DSA is essential.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

General Aptitude + Technical Ability Test

~60 min
Aptitude and CS fundamentals

The written round includes a general aptitude section (mathematical reasoning, logical reasoning, verbal ability) and a technical ability test covering OS, algorithms, data structures, C, Java, SQL, and networking.

Example questions
  • Mathematical and logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability
  • Data structures and algorithms MCQs
  • C/Java concepts and output prediction
  • SQL and networking fundamentals
How to prepare
  • Take timed aptitude mocks
  • Revise DS, algorithms, C/Java, SQL, and networking
  • Practice output-prediction questions
  • Drill reasoning and verbal
Common mistakes
  • Under-preparing the technical ability test
  • Weak fundamentals across subjects
  • Poor time management
What they look for
  • Balanced aptitude accuracy
  • Strong CS fundamentals
  • Speed
2

Coding Test

~90 min
Demanding hands-on coding

Typically three problems spanning easy, medium, and hard difficulty in about 90 minutes. Solid LeetCode-medium practice is generally sufficient, but the hard problem separates top candidates. Common patterns include sliding window, prefix sums, and hashing.

Example questions
  • Sliding-window or two-pointer array/string problem
  • Prefix-sum or hashing-based problem
  • Recursion/backtracking or dynamic programming
  • Graph or union-find style problem
  • Bit-manipulation or math-heavy problem
How to prepare
  • Solve 60+ problems weighted toward medium (about 60% medium, 30% easy, 10% hard)
  • Master sliding window, prefix sums, hashing, and DP patterns
  • Practice full problems in 20-40 min windows
  • Handle edge cases and I/O precisely
Common mistakes
  • Spending too long on the hard problem and losing the easy/medium points
  • Failing hidden test cases
  • Not practicing enough medium-level DSA
What they look for
  • Multiple problems fully solved
  • Strong pattern recognition
  • Efficient, correct code
3

Technical Interview

30-60 min
DSA depth, fundamentals, and projects

A deeper technical round covering DSA (often with live coding or dry-runs), CS fundamentals, and your projects. Nagarro expects genuine problem-solving, not memorized answers.

Example questions
  • Live DSA problem or optimization discussion
  • OOP, DBMS, and OS in depth
  • Project deep-dive and design choices
  • Complexity analysis of your solution
  • Debug or improve a given approach
How to prepare
  • Practice explaining your approach and complexity aloud
  • Revise DSA, OOP, DBMS, and OS deeply
  • Prepare thorough project explanations
  • Do mock technical interviews with follow-ups
Common mistakes
  • Jumping to code without explaining the approach
  • Weak complexity analysis
  • Overstating skills
What they look for
  • Clear problem-solving and communication
  • Strong DSA depth
  • Genuine project understanding
4

HR Interview

10-20 min
Fit and logistics

Covers motivation, communication, relocation, and joining. Usually the final, lighter step.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why Nagarro
  • Relocation and flexibility
  • Strengths, weaknesses, goals
  • Comfort with standard terms
How to prepare
  • Prepare a concise self-introduction
  • Research Nagarro's culture and business
  • Have consistent flexibility answers
  • Keep documents ready
Common mistakes
  • Generic answers
  • Inconsistency across rounds
  • Appearing inflexible
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Flexibility

What to master

  • Quantitative and logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability
  • Core DSA (sliding window, prefix sums, hashing, DP, graphs)
  • C and Java fundamentals
  • SQL and DBMS
  • Operating systems and networking
  • Complexity analysis
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

B.Tech/B.E in CSE, IT, ECE, or related streams; minimum 60% aggregate across 10th, 12th, and graduation; no backlogs at the time of application. Exact criteria vary by drive.

Frequently asked questions

How hard is Nagarro's coding round?

Genuinely demanding: about three problems spanning easy, medium, and hard. Strong LeetCode-medium practice is usually enough to clear it, with the hard problem separating top candidates.

How many written tests are there?

Typically two: a general aptitude plus technical ability test, and a separate coding test with three problems.

Which DSA patterns should I prioritize?

Sliding window, two pointers, prefix sums, hashing, recursion/backtracking, dynamic programming, and basic graphs/union-find.

How should I allocate coding-test time?

Secure the easy and medium problems first (roughly 20-30 min easy, 30-40 min medium) before attempting the hard one, so you do not lose guaranteed points.

What does the technical interview test?

Deeper DSA (often live), complexity analysis, CS fundamentals, and detailed project discussion. Explaining your approach clearly matters as much as the code.

Do backlogs matter?

You generally need no backlogs at the time of application. Confirm the exact rule for your drive.
