Mphasis Interview Guide (2026)

Mphasis hires freshers through an online aptitude test, a technical interview, an optional group discussion, and an HR round, followed by document verification. Its process is comparatively accessible: some drives accept candidates from any stream and are more lenient on backlogs, with strong communication skills carrying real weight.

Round-by-round: exactly what's asked & how to prepare

1

Online Aptitude Test

~60 min
Aptitude and basic technical screening

Covers quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability, and for technical roles some basic programming or technical questions. It is the primary elimination filter.

Example questions
  • Quantitative aptitude: arithmetic and DI
  • Logical reasoning: series and puzzles
  • Verbal ability and comprehension
  • Basic programming/technical MCQs (technical roles)
  • Grammar and vocabulary
How to prepare
  • Take timed aptitude mocks
  • Revise basic programming MCQs for technical roles
  • Practice reasoning and quant daily
  • Strengthen English
Common mistakes
  • Neglecting verbal
  • Poor time management
  • Ignoring basic coding for technical roles
What they look for
  • Balanced accuracy
  • Speed
  • Reasoning and verbal strength
2

Technical Interview

20-40 min
Fundamentals and projects

For technical roles, covers basic coding, one language, CS fundamentals, and your projects. Depth is moderate; clarity and communication matter as much as raw depth.

Example questions
  • Project walkthrough and your role
  • OOP concepts with examples
  • SQL/DBMS basics
  • A simple logic or coding question
  • Data structures you have used
How to prepare
  • Revise one language, OOP, and DBMS
  • Prepare project explanations
  • Practice basic SQL and simple coding
  • Do a mock technical interview
Common mistakes
  • Overstating resume skills
  • Weak project ownership
  • Poor communication
What they look for
  • Solid basics
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine project understanding
3

Group Discussion (when conducted)

15-20 min
Communication and collaboration

Some drives include a GD to assess how you articulate ideas, listen, and collaborate in a group. Not always present, but be ready for it.

Example questions
  • Current-affairs or general topic discussion
  • Abstract or opinion topics
  • Balancing contribution with listening
  • Structuring your points clearly
  • Constructive disagreement
How to prepare
  • Practice speaking clearly on general topics
  • Learn to enter and structure a GD
  • Read up on current affairs
  • Practice listening and building on others' points
Common mistakes
  • Dominating or staying silent
  • Being aggressive or dismissive
  • Speaking without substance
What they look for
  • Clear articulation
  • Active listening and collaboration
  • Structured thinking
4

HR Interview

10-20 min
Communication, fit, and logistics

Assesses communication, motivation, cultural fit, relocation, and joining logistics. Communication quality is weighted, since many roles are client-facing.

Example questions
  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why Mphasis and this role
  • Relocation and flexibility
  • Strengths, weaknesses, goals
  • Comfort with standard terms
How to prepare
  • Prepare a concise self-introduction
  • Research Mphasis's business
  • Have consistent flexibility answers
  • Keep documents ready for verification
Common mistakes
  • Weak spoken communication
  • Generic answers
  • Inconsistency across rounds
What they look for
  • Clear communication
  • Genuine motivation
  • Cultural fit

What to master

  • Quantitative aptitude
  • Logical reasoning
  • Verbal ability and communication
  • Basic programming and one language
  • OOP concepts
  • DBMS and SQL
  • MS Office basics (for some roles)
  • Resume project depth

Eligibility

Relatively flexible: some drives accept freshers from any stream and even candidates with backlogs, though technical roles expect basic coding. Good communication and basic MS Office knowledge are advantages. Exact criteria vary by role and drive.

Frequently asked questions

Is the process open to non-technical backgrounds?

Some Mphasis drives accept candidates from any stream, especially for operations/PMO roles, while technical roles expect basic coding knowledge.

Are backlogs a hard blocker?

Mphasis is comparatively lenient in some drives, and certain listings allow candidates with backlogs. Always confirm the specific drive's rules.

Is there a group discussion?

Some drives include a GD to assess communication and collaboration. Prepare for it in case it appears.

How important is communication?

Very. Many roles are client-facing, so spoken clarity and MS Office familiarity are genuine advantages in selection.

How long does hiring take?

The process is relatively quick, often around a couple of weeks on average depending on the role.

What roles do freshers get?

Common entry roles include Junior Trainee and similar, spanning technical and operations tracks depending on the drive.
