IBM India Interview Guide (2026)

Hires associate system/software engineers through a coding plus English online test, sometimes a GD, then technical and HR interviews.

ModerateOnline test → interviews (2–4 weeks)Updated Sep 2026

The hiring process

An online assessment combining aptitude, coding, and an English test shortlists candidates, sometimes with a group discussion, followed by technical and HR interviews. Each round is qualifying.

RoundFormatWhat's tested
Online assessment~90–120 minAptitude, coding, and English/cognitive sections
Group discussion (some drives)~15–20 minTeamwork and communication
Technical interview~30–45 minCoding, design patterns, CS theory, projects
HR interview~15–30 minCultural fit and communication

Process and cutoffs vary by drive and change over time — always confirm on the official careers page.

What to master

  • Aptitude and English
  • Coding in Java/C++/Python/Node.js
  • DSA and CS fundamentals
  • Design patterns basics
  • Clear spoken/written communication
  • Project explanation

Eligibility

Commonly minimum 60% or 6.0 CGPA with no active backlogs (some listings ask 65% in graduation/post-graduation); B.E./B.Tech in CSE/IT and related branches, often across recent batches. Varies by drive.

Your prep plan

2-week plan

  • Days 1–3: Aptitude and English/cognitive practice
  • Days 4–7: Daily coding in your main language (arrays, strings, DSA)
  • Days 8–10: CS fundamentals and design-pattern basics
  • Days 11–12: Communication and GD practice
  • Days 13–14: Full mock test plus mock technical and HR

2-day crash plan

  • Day 1: Timed aptitude/English plus 8–10 coding problems
  • Day 2: CS fundamentals revision, one GD topic, and a mock interview

FAQ

Is there an English test?

Yes. IBM's online assessment commonly includes an English/cognitive component alongside coding, so practice clear written and spoken communication.

Which languages are accepted for coding?

Reports mention Java, C++, Python, and Node.js among preferred languages. Use the one you are strongest in.

Is every round elimination?

Yes. Each stage is qualifying, so you advance only after clearing the current one.
